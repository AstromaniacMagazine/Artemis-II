2022 · Completed

The uncrewed test flight

Artemis I tested SLS and Orion on a mission around the Moon. It provided flight data and revealed issues, including unexpected heat-shield char loss, that needed attention before people flew.

People aboard, all the way around

The first crewed Artemis flight tested Orion’s systems, returned photographs and observations, and brought its four-person crew safely home.

2027 · Target

Rendezvous and docking in Earth orbit

NASA plans an Earth-orbit demonstration with test versions of one or both commercial human landing systems from Blue Origin and SpaceX. The prime crew is Randy Bresnik (commander), Luca Parmitano (pilot), Andre Douglas and Frank Rubio (mission specialists).

2028 · Target

The planned return to the surface

Artemis IV is planned to take astronauts back to the lunar surface, with a landing near the South Pole. The mission depends on the readiness of the systems and demonstrations that precede it.

Late 2028 · Target

Towards a lasting presence

NASA’s published plan anticipates another surface mission by late 2028 and the start of Moon-base construction during this phase. These remain programme targets, not confirmed launch dates.
