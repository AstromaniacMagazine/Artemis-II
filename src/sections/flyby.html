The flight path

Around the Moon. Back to Earth.

Artemis II was a flyby, not a lunar-orbit mission. Its free-return trajectory used lunar gravity to curve the spacecraft’s path back towards Earth.

  1. 01

    Launch

    SLS lifted off from Kennedy and placed Orion in Earth orbit.

  2. 02

    Earth-orbit checks

    The crew checked systems and practised manual handling before leaving Earth.

  3. 03

    Translunar injection

    The service module’s main engine sent Orion towards the Moon.

  4. 04

    Behind the Moon

    Orion flew past the far side without entering lunar orbit. The Moon blocked the radio link for about 40 minutes.

  5. 05

    Homeward flight

    The free-return trajectory curved back towards Earth. Course corrections refined the return path.

  6. 06

    Re-entry & recovery

    Orion separated from its service module, entered the atmosphere, deployed parachutes and splashed down in the Pacific.
