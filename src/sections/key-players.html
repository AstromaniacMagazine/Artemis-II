Behind the mission

Flown by four. Made possible by thousands.

The flight depended on people across NASA, its international partners and industry. These are some of the leaders and teams behind it; current and former roles are distinguished below.

Charlie Blackwell-Thompson

NASA’s first woman launch director, Charlie Blackwell-Thompson led the Artemis II countdown team at Kennedy.
