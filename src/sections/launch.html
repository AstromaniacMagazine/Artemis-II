Launch to lunar view

Five views worth slowing down for.

A rocket leaving Florida. Earth over a lunar horizon. A Sun hidden by the Moon. The mission unfolded in photographs as much as in milestones.

The sky caught fire

At 6:35 p.m. EDT on 1 April, SLS lifted off from Kennedy’s Launch Pad 39B. Inside Orion, four astronauts began the first crewed Artemis flight.

Liftoff

Earthset, from the other side

On 6 April, Earth slipped below the lunar horizon. The foreground is another world; the small, bright world beyond it is ours.

Closest approach

An eclipse beyond the Moon

From Orion, the Moon covered the Sun for almost an hour. The crew observed the solar corona and watched for flashes on the dark lunar surface.

Amaze, amaze, amaze

Life inside Integrity

Between the views were checklists, exercise, meals and spacecraft tests. The crew’s observations of life aboard Orion will help shape the missions that follow.

We will explore

Four names in the story

Wiseman, Glover, Koch and Hansen carried the first crewed Artemis mission around the Moon. Glover became the first person of colour, Koch the first woman, and Hansen the first non-American to make that journey.
