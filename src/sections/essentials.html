Back to the Moon. A new view of home.

Artemis II took Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen beyond low Earth orbit for the first time since Apollo 17 in 1972. Launched on 1 April 2026, their Orion spacecraft, Integrity, flew around the Moon and returned to the Pacific on 10 April.

This was a crewed flight test, with no lunar landing. Alongside the photographs came practical work: checking life support, flying Orion by hand, observing the lunar surface and bringing a crew safely through a lunar-speed return.
